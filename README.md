## 🌟 What is TeleDrive?

TeleDrive is a powerful Android application that transforms your private Telegram channel into your own personal cloud storage. Imagine having unlimited storage space for your photos, documents, and files—without paying for expensive cloud subscriptions. That's exactly what TeleDrive offers.

Whether you want to automatically back up your precious photos, securely store sensitive documents, or simply organize your files in a clean and intuitive interface, TeleDrive does it all. It's like having Dropbox or Google Drive, but powered by your Telegram account and completely under your control.

## ✨ Key Features

### 📸 Automatic Photo Backup
Never lose a precious memory again. TeleDrive automatically uploads your photos to your private Telegram channel as soon as you take them. Your memories are safe, organized, and accessible from any device.

### 🔒 End-to-End Encryption (Optional)
For those who prioritize privacy, TeleDrive offers optional end-to-end encryption. Your files are encrypted on your device before they ever leave it. Only you hold the keys to decrypt them. Even Telegram cannot read your encrypted files.

### 📂 Full-File Manager
Browse, organize, rename, move, and delete files with ease. The intuitive file manager gives you complete control over your cloud storage. Create folders, sort files, and find what you need in seconds.

### 🎨 Modern Material You Design
Built with Jetpack Compose and following Material You guidelines, TeleDrive looks beautiful and adapts to your device's theme. The interface is clean, responsive, and a joy to use.

### ⚡ Fast and Efficient
Powered by TDLib (Telegram Database Library), TeleDrive is optimized for speed and efficiency. Uploads and downloads are quick, and the app runs smoothly even on older devices.

### 🔄 Multi-Platform Access
Since your files are stored in a Telegram channel, you can access them from any device with Telegram installed—your phone, tablet, or computer.

## 🔧 Initial Setup

### Connecting Your Telegram Account

1. **Open TeleDrive** – You'll see a welcome screen.
2. **Tap "Sign in with Telegram"** – You'll be redirected to the Telegram login page.
3. **Enter your phone number** – Use the same number associated with your Telegram account.
4. **Verify your identity** – Enter the confirmation code sent to your Telegram app.
5. **Grant permissions** – Allow TeleDrive to access your photos and media (required for auto-backup).

### Creating Your Private Channel

1. **Within TeleDrive**, tap "Create New Channel" (or use an existing private channel).
2. **Name your channel** – Something like "My Personal Cloud" works well.
3. **Set the channel to Private** – This ensures only you can access it.
4. **Confirm** – TeleDrive will now use this channel as your cloud storage.

## 📖 How to Use TeleDrive

### 📤 Uploading Files Manually
- Open TeleDrive and tap the **+** button
- Choose files from your device
- They'll upload instantly to your channel

### 📥 Downloading Files
- Browse your files in the file manager
- Tap any file to preview it
- Tap the download icon to save it to your device

### 🔐 Enabling Encryption
- Go to **Settings** > **Security**
- Toggle "End-to-End Encryption" on
- Create a strong password (don't forget it—it cannot be recovered!)
- Your files will now be encrypted before upload

### 📂 Organizing Your Files
- Use the file manager to create folders
- Long-press any file to select it
- Tap "Move" to relocate files into folders

Your storage limit depends on your Telegram account. Telegram offers generous free storage, and you can upgrade for even more space.

Absolutely! Since your files are stored in a Telegram channel, you can access them via Telegram Web or the desktop app.

TeleDrive uses Telegram's secure infrastructure. With optional end-to-end encryption, your files are protected with military-grade encryption before they leave your device.

## 🛠️ Troubleshooting

### App Won't Install
- Ensure you have enough storage space
- Check that "Unknown Sources" is enabled
- Try downloading the file again

### Can't Sign In
- Verify your phone number is correct
- Check your internet connection
- Make sure you have the latest version of Telegram

### Photos Not Backing Up Automatically
- Check that auto-backup is enabled in Settings
- Ensure TeleDrive has storage permission
- Verify your battery optimization settings aren't killing the app
